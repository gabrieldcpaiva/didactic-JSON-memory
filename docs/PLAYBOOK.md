# Playbook: turning this repo into money or saved hours

Two paths. Most people should do both.

- **Path A — sell the output.** You are the operator. The system is your edge on speed and consistency.
- **Path B — sell the system.** You are the toolmaker. The repo becomes a product, a course, or a retainer.

Cost floor per delivered image is roughly $0.13–0.24 in model fees. Everything above that is your pricing decision.

---

## Path A — sell the output

### 1. Local catalog refresh

**Who pays:** shops with a marketplace listing and bad photos.
**Offer:** "20 product photos, same style, 5 days."
**Why it works:** you shoot nothing. The owner sends phone photos; `product-hero-shot` does the rest.
**Price anchor:** one studio day. You deliver in one afternoon.

### 2. Thumbnail retainer for creators

**Who pays:** channels between 5k and 200k subscribers, posting weekly.
**Offer:** 8 thumbnails/month, two headline variants each, 48h turnaround.
**Why it works:** `youtube-thumbnail` plus batch mode makes the marginal thumbnail ~4 minutes.
**Price anchor:** a junior editor's day rate, charged monthly.

### 3. Notes-to-one-pager for consultants

**Who pays:** coaches, agencies, B2B founders who write long and publish rarely.
**Offer:** "Send notes. Get a branded one-page visual by Friday."
**Why it works:** `notes-to-infographic` needs only their words. The lock list protects their numbers, which is the actual fear.

### 4. Photo restoration for families

**Who pays:** people with one irreplaceable damaged photo.
**Offer:** tiered by damage: light / heavy / colourise.
**Why it works:** high emotional value, low competition at the low end, and `photo-repair-and-light` prevents the "different person" failure that makes people distrust AI restoration.

### 5. Listing photo cleanup for realtors

**Who pays:** agents with 25–60 photos per listing.
**Offer:** flat fee per listing, 24h.
**Caution:** some marketplaces require disclosure of edited photos. Tell your client, in writing.

### 6. Export campaign kit

**Who pays:** small exporters entering a new market.
**Offer:** one campaign, five languages, identical layout.
**Why it works:** `localized-ad-set` uses the first language as a master, which is exactly what brand teams ask for and rarely get.

## Path B — sell the system

### 7. Template pack

Sell the filled JSON packages plus a one-page checklist.
**Price:** low, volume-driven. **Risk:** easy to copy. **Defence:** your memory file, which is personal and grows.

### 8. Workshop

"One afternoon, your own image system."
Deliverables: their brand style plate, five templates filled with their data, a memory file seeded with their lessons.
**Price:** a day rate. **Why it sells:** they leave with an asset, not notes.

### 9. Consistency audit

**Who pays:** brands whose images drift across channels.
**Deliverable:** a style plate, a drift report with side-by-side evidence, and the rules that fix it.
**Why it is non-obvious:** everyone sells more images. Almost nobody sells the *reason* images stop matching.

### 10. Internal standard for an agency

License the repo plus a private memory file as the agency's house prompt standard.
**Recurring:** quarterly memory review. **Sticky:** their lessons live in your format.

### 11. Micro-SaaS or internal tool

The renderer is 200 lines. Wrap it in a form. Non-technical staff pick a template, fill five fields, get a prompt. The memory runs behind the scenes.
**Charge per seat**, not per prompt. The value is consistency, not generation.

## Pricing logic that survives contact with clients

| You sell | They compare it to | Charge for |
|---|---|---|
| Finished images | A photoshoot | The reshoot they avoid |
| Turnaround | Their own evenings | Time returned |
| Consistency | A brand guideline project | The drift they cannot see |
| The system | A course | The lessons they would learn slowly |

Never price against model cost. Nobody buys tokens. They buy a result they could not make.

---

## The efficiency argument, in numbers

| Task | Manual | With this repo |
|---|---|---|
| First draft of a prompt | 10–20 min | 30 sec |
| Same prompt for 8 products | 2 hours | 6 min (batch) |
| Remembering last month's fix | Luck | Memory file |
| Onboarding a helper | Explain everything | Send `docs/GUIDE.md` |
| Cost per accepted image | 4 drafts at 4K ≈ $0.96 | 3 at 1K + 1 at 2K ≈ $0.54 |

The last row is not the big win. The big win is the third one: **the system remembers so you do not have to.**

## What to keep private

- Your memory file. It is your actual moat.
- Your client-specific locks (label text, legal lines).
- Your failure list for a niche. Knowing exactly how cosmetics packaging fails is worth more than the template.
